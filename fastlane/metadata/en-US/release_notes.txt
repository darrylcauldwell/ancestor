Initial release.

- Research pipeline with 7 sources (FreeBMD, FreeCen, CWGC, Find a Grave, Probate, Wirksworth, FreeREG)
- 4-gate record scorer with life clustering
- GPS scoring per the Genealogical Proof Standard
- Interactive pedigree and descendants tree views
- 18 audit rules for consistency and gap detection
- WikiTree and GEDCOM import
- Local reasoning model support (Apple Silicon)
- Evidence Firewall ensuring all findings are verified before entering the tree
